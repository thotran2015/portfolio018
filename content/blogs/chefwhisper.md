I built [Chef Whisper](https://chefwhisper.org) so cooking from the web is less noisy. Paste a recipe link and get the ingredients and method, without the ads, pop-ups, or long personal essays.

## How it works

1. Open [chefwhisper.org](https://chefwhisper.org).
2. Paste a recipe URL.
3. Extract (free, no account required).

Sign in if you want to save recipes to a personal library.

## Extra features

- **Voice-guided cooking mode**: step through the recipe hands-free with simple voice commands.
- **Browser extension**: extract from pages without copying the URL around.

Try it at [https://chefwhisper.org](https://chefwhisper.org).
